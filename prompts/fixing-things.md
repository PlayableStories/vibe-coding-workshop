# When Something Breaks (or You're Just Lost)

Nothing here can't be undone, and the AI is genuinely good at getting you unstuck. Stay calm, describe what you see, and let it fix things. You never need to read or edit code yourself.

## The magic words

> Undo that last change — I preferred it before.

> Go back to how it was before my last few changes.

## It won't run / there's an error

> The game won't run. Here's what I see: [describe it, or paste any red text]. Please fix it so it runs again. I'm not a coder.

> Something broke after my last change. Can you fix it while keeping the change I wanted?

> Just get it back to a working state, even if we lose the last thing I tried.

## It runs, but looks wrong

> The game runs but [the text is cut off / the buttons overlap / I can't read the words / it's off the side of the screen]. Please fix the layout.

> It looks broken on my phone. Make it work on a small screen too.

> The colours I asked for are hard to read. Adjust them so there's enough contrast.

## It doesn't do what I meant

> That's not what I meant. I wanted [describe again, differently]. Let's try that.

> You changed more than I asked. I only wanted [the one thing] — please put the rest back.

## I'm lost / I don't understand

> Remind me in plain English what this game does right now and what we last changed.

> I'm overwhelmed. What's one small thing I could change next that would make a nice difference?

> Explain what just happened like I'm not a coder — because I'm not.

## When to wave someone over

If you've asked the AI to fix the same thing two or three times and it's still wrong, don't keep wrestling it — **call your facilitator over.** A fresh pair of eyes solves it in seconds, and you'll have plenty of building left to do.
