# The Prompt Library

For a non-coder, **your prompts are your code.** The sentences you say to the AI are the real material you're working with — so here are the ones that work, ready to copy, paste, and adapt.

A few habits that make every prompt land better:

- **Say the *why*, not just the *what*.** "Make the colours sad" is okay. "Make the colours feel like a faded old photograph, because the game is about a memory slipping away" is much better. The AI designs well when it knows the feeling you're chasing.
- **One change at a time.** Ask, look at the result, then ask for the next thing. It's far easier to fix.
- **Talk to it like a helpful colleague,** not a search engine. Full sentences. "I didn't like that, can we try…"
- **You're allowed to undo.** Nothing is permanent.

The three lists:

- **[Reskin prompts](reskin-prompts.md)** — change the words and the look (Rung 1)
- **[Remix prompts](remix-prompts.md)** — change a rule, change the meaning (Rung 2)
- **[Fixing things](fixing-things.md)** — when something breaks, looks wrong, or you're lost

> 🪄 In every prompt, the bits in `[square brackets]` are for you to fill in.
