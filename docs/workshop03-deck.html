Games that mean something

Passage

A narrow strip. You always drift right. Take a spouse and you move at half speed but score double. Five minutes, then you die.

You can't see far ahead, you can't go back, and love costs you mobility.

Loneliness

A small black square. Approach the others and they scatter. No goal, no ending.

Reaching out is what makes them leave.

Dumb Ways to Die

Cheerful cartoon deaths, a song you can't shake, then: stand behind the yellow line.

A safety message people chose to share.

Florence

Conversations are jigsaw pieces. Early on, many and fiddly. As the relationship settles, two pieces. Later, hard again.

Talking to someone gets easier, then it doesn't.

What the conversation sounds like
  • "I want to make a game about ____. Before we build anything, ask me questions."
  • "What familiar game is closest to this feeling?"
  • "Where does this idea fall apart? Push back."
  • "Write this up as a build prompt for Claude Code. One HTML file, the smallest playable version."

The thinking happens in the chat. The prompt is what you carry across.

Wherever you are, it counts
  1. Reskin — keep the rules, change the words and the look. Most people start here, and it's plenty.
  2. Remix — find the expectation the game relies on, and break it.
  3. Realise — describe a whole new game from a new idea.

Which rung are you on? Getting a first one working is a finished game.

Publish

Get a link you can send to anyone.

  1. Make sure it runs and plays the way you want.
  2. In Replit, press Deploy or Publish and pick the free option. Building in Claude Code? Upload the HTML file to itch.io.
  3. Open the link on your phone. Hand it to your neighbour without explaining it.
