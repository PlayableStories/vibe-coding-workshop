# Before You Arrive

Five minutes of setup, ideally done at home the night before. Nothing to install. Nothing to pay for.

## 1. Make a free Replit account

Replit is the tool we'll build in. It runs entirely in your web browser — there's nothing to download.

1. Go to **[replit.com](https://replit.com)**
2. Click **Sign up** (Google or email both work)
3. That's it. You're done.

You don't need to know what any of the buttons do yet. We'll get there together.

## 2. Bring a thing you care about

This matters more than the account.

The best games made in this workshop come from people who walked in with *something on their mind* — a frustration, a memory, a worry, a small injustice, a feeling they couldn't quite name. The game becomes a way to say it.

So before you arrive, jot down a few of these on your phone:

- Something about your work or industry that feels absurd
- A memory of a place or a person
- Something in the news you can't stop thinking about
- A feeling you wish other people understood

You don't have to use any of them. But it's much easier to start from "I want to say *this*" than from a blank screen.

## 3. (Optional) Have a look at the games

If you're curious, skim the **[catalogue](02-pick-a-game.md)** and play one or two of the games. You'll pick one to start from, and it helps to arrive with a favourite.
