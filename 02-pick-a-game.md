# Pick a Game

These five games are your starting points. Each one already works, already means something, and is built to be remade. You'll pick **one** and make it yours.

Don't agonise. Pick the one whose *feeling* is closest to the thing you want to say — you can always change everything on top of it.

**How to read each entry:**
- **The story it tells** — the one-sentence meaning baked into the rules
- **The mechanic that carries it** — *how* the rule creates the meaning (this is the bit you'll bend in a Remix)
- **▶ Play** — try it first (ask your facilitator for the live link if one isn't here yet)
- **🔗 Import URL** — paste this into Replit to start from it (see [Reskin](03-reskin.md))
- **Best for** — which rung this game is friendliest for
- **Remix sparks** — ideas to get you going

## 🏢 Boardroom
*A satire of corporate leadership — swipe left or right on impossible decisions.*

- **The story it tells:** Leadership is an impossible balancing act; every choice pleases someone and betrays someone else.
- **The mechanic that carries it:** Four meters (Shareholders, Management, Staff, Public Image) all start at 50. Every decision moves them in opposite directions. Push any one too far and your empire collapses in one of nine ways.
- **▶ Play:** _(add live link)_
- **🔗 Import URL:** `https://github.com/PlayableStories/boardroom-game`
- **Best for:** Reskin (its text and dilemmas live in one file — very easy to rewrite). Great Remix too.
- **Remix sparks:** Make it a head teacher, a hospital ward, a parent of teenagers, a small-town mayor, a group chat admin. Rename the four meters to the four things *your* world pulls between.

## 🪑 Office Chair Racing Championship
*The tech-career treadmill, made literal — race office chairs down a corridor.*

- **The story it tells:** Climbing the ladder doesn't make you safe; every promotion just brings faster, scarier competition.
- **The mechanic that carries it:** You pump the arrow keys to go faster. Overtake someone and you get promoted; get overtaken and you're demoted. The moment you stop, you're fired. Every promotion makes your rivals more aggressive.
- **▶ Play:** _(add live link)_
- **🔗 Import URL:** `https://github.com/PlayableStories/office-race-game`
- **Best for:** Reskin (rename the ranks and re-theme the world). Remix is more advanced here — it's an arcade engine.
- **Remix sparks:** Swap "Junior Developer → CEO" for any ladder: understudy → lead actor, intern → partner, apprentice → master. Or change what "stopping" means.

## 💊 Pill Match
*Taking medicine safely is about restraint, not greed.*

- **The story it tells:** Do the right amount, in the right order — more is not better, more can be fatal.
- **The mechanic that carries it:** A Match-3 game flipped on its head. Only the *prescribed* colour earns points; matching the wrong colour *costs* you, and your score can go negative. Take double the dose and you lose.
- **▶ Play:** _(add live link)_
- **🔗 Import URL:** `https://github.com/PlayableStories/pill-match`
- **Best for:** A brilliant *example* of a Remix (it's Match-3 with one rule inverted). Reskin the theme onto another "restraint" story.
- **Remix sparks:** Any place where *more* is the trap: doomscrolling, overspending, overwatering a plant, saying yes to too much. What's the "wrong colour" in that world?

## 🏠 Memory of Home
*A memory can be true without being whole.*

- **The story it tells:** When we remember a place, we keep fragments — the shape of a thing, or how it felt, rarely both.
- **The mechanic that carries it:** A memory-matching game where two cards match if they share the same *object* **or** the same *colour* — never both. No score, no timer, no losing. Each match reveals a fragment of writing.
- **▶ Play:** _(add live link)_
- **🔗 Import URL:** `https://github.com/PlayableStories/memory-of-home`
- **Best for:** The gentlest, most personal starting point. Lovely for Reskin (swap the objects, colours, and fragments for your own memory).
- **Remix sparks:** Your childhood kitchen, a grandparent's house, a city you left, a relationship that ended. What six objects and four colours hold it?

## 🌗 Half of Me
*When you lose your home, you keep the place or the feeling — not both.*

- **The story it tells:** A personal story of displacement that points outward to climate-driven loss of home.
- **The mechanic that carries it:** A Level-2 *Remix* of Memory of Home — every match gives you only "half a memory." It's the proof of this whole workshop: one rule changed, an entire new meaning.
- **▶ Play:** _(add live link — `submission-vibecoding` branch)_
- **🔗 Import URL:** `https://github.com/PlayableStories/half-of-me` _(use the `submission-vibecoding` branch)_
- **Best for:** Study this one as the *model* of a great Remix before you do your own.
- **Remix sparks:** Look at what it changed from Memory of Home, then ask: what would *your* "half" be?

## Can't decide?

- Want to **say something funny / angry about work or society** → **Boardroom**
- Want to say something about **pressure and burnout** → **Office Chair Racing**
- Want to make a point about **doing the right amount of something** → **Pill Match**
- Want to make something **tender and personal** → **Memory of Home**
- Want to see **the perfect Remix** before trying your own → **Half of Me**
